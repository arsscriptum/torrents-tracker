About Torrents-Tracker Application

Torrents-Tracker is Django-based web application that I wrote to facilitate searching for movies, TV Shows, and other medias through popular torrent indexing services like "The Pirate Bay (TPB)" and "1337x".

The main advantages of using this tool are:
• Integrated VPN: you don't need to worry about hiding your search requests from you ISP. If you use a VPN service, you may forget to enable it, with this tool, the VPN is active for all the request made by the tool
• No ads, no trackers, just pure data: you will not be presented with any ads nor any data you don't want while using Torrents-Tracker
